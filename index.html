FAQ

Common questions

Is LEX giving me legal advice?▼
No. LEX provides legal information only — not legal advice. There is an important difference. Legal information explains what the law says. Legal advice applies that law to your specific situation and is provided by a licensed attorney. LEX does the former. For significant matters, you should always consult a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.
Which countries does LEX cover?▼
LEX covers 16 jurisdictions: United States, United Kingdom, European Union, Australia, Canada, Singapore, India, Germany, France, UAE, South Africa, Japan, Brazil, Mexico, New Zealand and Spain. We cover Common Law, Civil Law and Sharia-influenced legal systems.
How accurate are LEX's answers?▼
LEX cites real statutes and legislation so you can verify every answer independently. Like any AI system, it can make mistakes and may not reflect the most recent legal changes. Always verify important information and consult a lawyer for high-stakes situations.
Is my data private and secure?▼
Yes. Your conversations are private to your account and are not used to train AI models. We do not sell your data to third parties. Uploaded documents are processed in real-time and not stored after your session ends.
Can I cancel my subscription?▼
Yes, at any time. Visit the customer portal via the Manage Subscription button in your account, or email lexaillc1@gmail.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. We offer a 7-day refund on your first payment.
What is the Business plan for?▼
The Business plan is designed for law firms, HR departments, letting agencies, and organisations that want to give their team or clients access to LEX. It includes team seats, white label options, a client-facing portal, custom practice areas, usage reporting, and a dedicated onboarding call. Contact us at lexaillc1@gmail.com to discuss your needs.
